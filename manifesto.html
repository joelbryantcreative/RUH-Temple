RŪḤ ← Return

Manifesto

Some things cannot be explained.
Only felt.

Some things are older than language —
smoke rising from burning resin,
the warmth of skin after sunlight,
the smell of a place you have never been
but somehow remember.

Some things are lost when you leave home.
And some things follow you across every ocean,
every season, every year of forgetting.

Some things are made in laboratories.
Others are made from longing.

Some things demand your attention.
The ones worth keeping never do.

Some things are trends.
Some things are ancient.

Some things are purchased.
Some things are carried.

Some fragrances sit on the surface.
RŪḤ settles into skin — and stays.

Some things cannot be explained.

Only worn.
