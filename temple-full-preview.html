02 — The Vessel

An Artifact,
Not a Bottle

In ancient workshops, objects were made one at a time. Held, shaped, considered. The maker's hand left in every surface.

RŪḤ returns to that. Each vessel is cast by hand, in small batches — heavy, imperfect, irreplaceable. An object that carries the memory of being made.

The Founder

Heritage

Every RŪḤ fragrance is rooted in her heritage
The smoky warmth of Algerian interiors,
The refined sensibility of French perfumery.

Two cultures.
One soul.

RŪḤ was not built from a business plan.
It was built from longing.

She is half Algerian, half French —
far from both for longer than she expected.
Distance does something to a person.
It doesn't erase where you come from.
It sharpens it.

Scent was always the thread.
The warmth of a house in Algeria.
Smoke and resin, something ancient in the air.
The kind of smell that doesn't announce itself —
it simply is, the way memory is.

She searched for that feeling in a bottle for years.
Through every major house, every niche brand she could find.
Nothing came close.
So she made it herself.

RŪḤ — soul in Arabic —
was named in honour of her Algerian heritage.
Not as aesthetic.
As devotion.

Every fragrance is clean, vegan-friendly, and plastic-free
because it was never negotiable.

RŪḤ Origine is the fragrance
she spent years not finding.

A feeling you wear.
